# BLOCKERS — ShareTrip

**Things that need a person.** Each one says what is blocked, the exact remedy, and **what
was built instead** — so nothing was left waiting on somebody's inbox.

Split out of [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md) at the Standard tier; the `B-` ids did not change.

A blocker is not a complaint, and it is not a to-do. It is a claim that work cannot proceed
without a specific act by a specific person, plus the proof that you did not just stop.

**Status changes are prepended as blockquotes above the original text, which is never
edited.** Half of all blockers stop mattering once the workaround exists, and the record of
*that* is more useful than a tidy file.
